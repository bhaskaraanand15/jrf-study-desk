Pre-launch checklist

Draft for site-owner review · Last reviewed 30 October 2026

This project is a deployment candidate, not a declaration of legal clearance. Do not announce or promote the website publicly until every required owner action below is complete.

Identity and domain

  • Choose and register a custom domain.
  • Configure the domain in GitHub Pages and verify HTTPS.
  • Add the exact custom domain to the repository's Pages settings and DNS records.
  • Confirm the final canonical URL and update all policy links if needed.

Owner and contact details

  • Insert the operator's legal name or entity name.
  • Add a monitored privacy/grievance contact email.
  • Confirm whether a postal address or other business details must be displayed for the actual operating model.
  • Have an Indian legal professional review the privacy policy, terms and applicable consumer/e-commerce requirements.

Technical checks

  • Review favicon.svg and replace it if it conflicts with an existing mark.
  • Run keyboard-only navigation and screen-reader checks.
  • Test contrast for every text/control state and all responsive breakpoints.
  • Check all internal links, backups, restore and reset flows.
  • Confirm no analytics, trackers, remote fonts, embeds or third-party scripts have been introduced.
  • Review GitHub Pages repository visibility and source-code exposure before publishing personal or proprietary material.

Legal and content checks

  • Review current DPDP Act commencement notifications and Rules before the May 2027 phase.
  • Verify every syllabus item against the current official source.
  • Remove unsupported claims, invented weightage, fabricated PYQs, testimonials and user counters.
  • Check licences and attribution for any third-party text, logos, diagrams or images. This release uses no decorative stock photography.
  • Do not collect data through forms until a clear notice, necessary fields, purpose, retention, contact route and appropriate consent/other lawful basis are implemented.
