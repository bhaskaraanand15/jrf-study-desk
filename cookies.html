Cookie and Local Storage Policy

Draft for site-owner review · Last reviewed 30 October 2026

1. Cookies

The current website code does not intentionally set HTTP cookies, advertising cookies or analytics cookies. No cookie-consent banner is configured because no non-essential tracking cookies or third-party tracking tools are included in this release.

2. Local browser storage

The study tracker uses browser local storage to remember topic completion, notes, revision dates, PYQ counts and study preferences. Local storage is not technically a cookie, but it stores information on your device. The information remains in the browser profile until you reset it, clear site data or remove the browser profile.

3. Your controls

Use the site's export and reset controls or your browser's site-data settings to manage local information. Exported backups may contain personal notes and should be kept secure.

4. Future changes

If analytics, advertising, embedded media, external widgets or other storage technologies are added, this policy must be updated and any legally required consent mechanism must be implemented before those technologies operate. Reassess applicable Indian law and other laws based on the audience and deployment location.
