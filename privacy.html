Privacy Policy

Draft for site-owner review · Last reviewed 30 October 2026

1. Who operates this website

This independent study-planning website is operated by [INSERT LEGAL NAME OF OWNER] (“we”, “us”). Contact: [INSERT A MONITORED PRIVACY / GRIEVANCE EMAIL]. Do not publish until these details are completed.

2. What this website stores

The current static version does not ask you to create an account and does not request your name, phone number, email address, date of birth, location or payment details. Study progress, topic notes, PYQ counts and preferences are saved in local browser storage on the device you use. These entries are not transmitted to the site operator by the website code.

Do not enter passwords, financial information, health information or another person's personal information into study notes. Anyone with access to your browser profile or device may be able to view locally saved information.

3. Hosting and technical logs

The website is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses, request details and security logs as part of providing its hosting service. GitHub operates independently under its own terms and privacy documentation. Review GitHub Pages information and GitHub's Privacy Statement.

4. Analytics, advertising and third-party content

The current release contains no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, behavioural tracking, social widgets, video embeds, remote fonts or third-party form embeds. External links open the linked provider's website; that provider's privacy practices apply after you leave this site.

5. Purpose and retention

Local browser storage is used only to remember your study checklist, notes, schedule preferences and locally entered question counts. You control this information through the website's export, import and reset controls, and through your browser settings. Clearing site data may permanently remove it. Exported backup files are under your control and may contain your notes.

6. Your choices and requests

You can use the site without creating an account. You can delete locally stored progress using the reset control or browser site-data settings. For privacy questions or requests concerning information processed by the operator, contact [INSERT PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL]. This draft must be updated if accounts, cloud sync, forms, analytics, payments or other data collection are added.

7. Children

This study tool is not designed to collect information from children. Do not submit information about a child through notes or other fields.

8. India data protection

We intend to operate in a data-minimising manner and to review the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 as their provisions commence. Applicability and obligations depend on actual processing, the operator and the relevant commencement dates. This policy is not a representation that all legal obligations have been independently certified.

We will update this page when the website's data practices change. The date at the top should be updated after each substantive revision.
